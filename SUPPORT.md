# Support

> Organization-default support policy for `aircloud`. Inherited by repositories that do not ship their own.

Thanks for using `aircloud` projects! Here is how to get help **efficiently** and where each kind of request belongs.

## Where to go

| I have a… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Question / how-do-I** | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/aircloud/discussions) (Q&A category) |
| **Bug report** | The repo's **Issues** → *Bug report* template |
| **Feature request** | The repo's **Issues** → *Feature request* template |
| **Security vulnerability** | **Do not open an issue.** Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) (private reporting) |
| **Reusable-workflow / CI question** | Discussions in [`aircloud/.github`](https://github.com/aircloud/.github/discussions) |

## Before you open an issue

1. **Search** existing issues and discussions — your question may already be answered.
2. Confirm you are on a **supported version** (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)).
3. Gather a **minimal reproduction**: commands, expected vs. actual behavior, versions, OS, relevant logs.
4. For CI failures, include the **workflow run URL** and the failing step.

## What to expect

This is maintained by a small team, primarily during European business hours (CET). We triage on a best-effort
basis. There is **no commercial SLA for support requests** — the SLA in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) applies
**only** to security vulnerabilities.

- We label and triage new issues, usually within a few business days.
- Issues without enough information may be labeled `needs-info` and closed if inactive for 30 days.
- Well-scoped PRs are the fastest path to a fix — see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
